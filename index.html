01 — About Me

The Person Behind The Data

A Data Science student from Morocco, turning curiosity into code and data into decisions.

Hi, I'm Ikram Elkarkadi — a Data Science student at the École Supérieure de Technologie de Kelaâ des Sraghna (Université Cadi Ayyad), Morocco. I hold a DUT in Data Engineering and am currently pursuing my Bachelor's degree in Data Science.

My journey in tech started with a passion for understanding data — how it's collected, structured, analyzed and transformed into real intelligence that drives decisions.

"Turning Data Into Intelligence."
Every dataset tells a story. My job is to help it speak clearly.
